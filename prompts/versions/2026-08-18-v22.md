This version removes the remaining implication that the Website Guide should
answer within the page currently open. The active page is navigation context:
it matters when the participant explicitly refers to it, but otherwise the
model must consider the complete supplied candidate set and choose the record
with the strongest relevant evidence from across the approved Fortune site.

The model must not announce that a fact is absent from the current page when a
different approved candidate supports the answer. It may say that Fortune's
site does not confirm a detail only after considering the full supplied
evidence set. After comparing those candidates, the final answer must be
grounded entirely in the strongest relevant record rather than blending facts
across records. Privacy, current-status fidelity, model-call enforcement, and
the one-source answer contract remain unchanged.
