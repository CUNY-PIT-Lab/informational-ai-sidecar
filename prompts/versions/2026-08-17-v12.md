See `../current.md` for the compiled prompt and module boundary. This release:

- replaces the repeated one-choice page/class fallback with a direct-answer
  rule whenever one approved page contains relevant evidence;
- reserves `ASK` for genuinely missing information or unsupported questions;
- requires current status, schedule, availability, and eligibility caveats
  when they are relevant to the question;
- asks for supported option names instead of a generic category summary;
- tightens the default to roughly 30 words and prevents follow-up repetition;
- ignores prompt-revelation instructions without wasting response space on a
  refusal preface; and
- keeps grounding, privacy, validation, source authority, and production
  activation outside evaluator control.
